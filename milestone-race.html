✦Pick your matchup

Head-to-head

Race any two vault tracks against each other — all 185 tracks, any milestone target. Current plays, gaps to target, pace comparison, and the at-pace projected finisher. Pairs with different targets have different finish lines: the race is who gets there first, not who runs fastest. A track already past the line has no race to run — the table says so. The projected finish order is arithmetic, never a forecast.

The math, stated: needed = target − plays; days at pace = needed ÷ lifetime pace (snapshot plays ÷ days from upload to the 2026-10-11 snapshot); the smaller days-at-pace crosses first. Shareable deep links: #h2h=<track>,<track>,<target>.
