Fish Narc — the man otherwise known as Ben Funkhouser, producer on two archive tracks — returns with his fifth solo LP, produced with Asheville's Alex Farrar (Wednesday, MJ Lenderman, Fiddlehead), continuing the fuzzy, jangly college-rock direction of 2025's frog song.

✦the singles

Out now

  • 'Much Less Me' — lead single (2026-08-18), with a Super 8 music video shot by Jolie M-A capturing his final days in Olympia.
  • 'Big Dumb World' — second single (2026-09-15), video by James Hyson.

On the title, the artist's own framing (Stereogum interview): saying 'uncle' — giving up — as a tongue-in-cheek nod; the record reflects on the last ten years, expectations, and aging, made from a mid-30s vantage point as a best-shot project rather than a youth-lane chase.
