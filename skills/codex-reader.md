# Read a codex family deep-dive

## Use when

An agent (or a visitor) wants the full story of one of the 16 codex word
families — not just which tracks carry a word (that's `transmission-decoder`),
but the family's decoded reading, how it evolved across the six eras, which
upload sessions carried it, and which collaborators named it. Feeds the
`codex/<slug>.html` deep-dive pages.

## Inputs

- A family name or slug (e.g. `The Modes`, `the-rites`), or a codex word to
  resolve to its family first (e.g. `nitemode` → The Modes)

## Procedure

1. Fetch `api/v1/codex-families.json`. It holds 16 families, each with `name`,
   `slug`, `epithet`, `words[]`, `page` (`codex/<slug>.html`), `track_count`,
   `total_plays`, and the deep-dive blocks below.
2. Read `decoded` FIRST — it is the family's official reading inside this
   archive: `status` is `"stated"` (the reading is the `epithet`, quoted
   verbatim) or `"unknown"` (the archive assigns no meaning — currently only
   The Modes' `litemode`/`nitemode`, per the unknowns register on
   `method.html`). **Never supply a meaning the archive doesn't claim.**
3. Walk `eras[]` for the evolution: each era carries `transmissions`,
   `plays`, the `loudest` family track, plus `deep_dive` (`eras/<id>.html`)
   and `journey` (`transmission.html#era-<id>`) links. `first_appearance`
   and `latest` bookend the family.
4. `transmissions[]` is every family track (title, date, plays, type,
   release-page link), chronological. `key_sessions[]` holds the upload
   sessions carrying family tracks (session deep-dive links where one
   exists, else `sessions.html`); distributor releases have no session
   record — say so, don't invent one.
5. `collaborators[]` names the title-credited voices on family tracks
   (`name`, `page` → `circle/<slug>.html`, family transmissions + plays).
   Credits are parsed from `w/`/`ft.` title fragments — as credited,
   verbatim; producers excluded.
6. Present: **the reading** (stated or honestly unknown), **the evolution**
   (first → latest across eras), **the transmissions**, **the sessions**,
   **the voices** — every figure tracing to an endpoint field.

## Worked example

Input: `the-rites`

- `GET api/v1/codex-families.json` → family **The Rites** —
  17 transmissions, 11,052 plays, words `ritual`, `solstice`, `freestyle`,
  `anthem`, `intro`, `outro`, `interlude`.
- `decoded.status` = `"stated"`: "The ceremonial forms: ritual, solstice,
  freestyle, anthem, intro, outro, interlude."
- Evolution: first appearance "Fuck Time 3 (outro) [Prod. DIX2]"
  (2018-11-08) → latest "Sti11 waiting/tunnel freestyle w/ Max Ferrari
  prod. suli" (2025-11-11); active in 5 of 6 eras — The First Rite ×6
  (8,099 plays), The Prolific Year ×2, The Cast It: Years ×1, The
  SomeLowLifes Era ×7 (2,243 plays — the most-played codex family of that
  era), The Silence ×1.
- Key sessions: 9, led by The November 3rd–12th Session ×6 family
  transmissions; voices: Yung Vetta ×1, Max Ferrari ×1 (title credits only).
- Page: `codex/the-rites.html`; journey links: `transmission.html#era-<id>`
  per active era.

## API references

- `api/v1/codex-families.json` — the 16 family deep-dives (endpoint #18)
- `api/v1/codex.json` — the raw codex: families, word lists, track lists
- `codex/<slug>.html` — the human deep-dive pages (generated by
  `scripts/build_codex_pages.py`)
- `decoder.html` + `assets/js/decoder.js` — word → family resolution
- `api/v1/index.json` — registry

## Pitfalls

- The `decoded.reading` is the ONLY meaning you may state. For The Modes
  the archive explicitly assigns no meaning (`litemode`/`nitemode` are
  tallied, never translated) — don't fill the silence.
- A reading is the archive's framing of title vocabulary, not an artist
  statement. Present it that way.
- Matching is title-text only. **Never quote or interpret lyrics** — the
  codex counts words in titles, nothing else.
- Distributor releases have no session record and no vault play count —
  don't attach them to sessions or invent plays for them.
