✦The scene record

Verified beyond the titles

Research pass 2026-10-12 — public sources only, no biographical invention, no lyrics.

  • Born 1992; Seattle/Olympia, Washington.
  • Member of Thraxxhouse and GothBoiClique.
  • Produced Lil Peep's 'Goth Angel Sinner' LP.
  • Worked with Wicca Phase Springs Eternal, Lil Tracy, Horse Head, and Cold Hart; production noted for live guitar instead of samples.
  • Solo LPs: 'WiLDFiRE' (2020), 'Camouflage' (2022), 'fruiting body' [KLP295] (2023), 'frog song' (2025, K Records).
  • Fifth solo LP 'Uncle' announced (Stereogum, August 2026): 11 tracks on K Records [KLP313], scheduled release 2026-10-16; produced by Asheville's Alex Farrar (Wednesday, MJ Lenderman, Fiddlehead).
  • Tracklist: 'Untitled', 'Much Less Me', 'Emma', 'Student Driver', 'One Hand On The Sword', 'In The Woods', 'Big Dumb World', 'Not To Guess', "Wouldn't You Know", 'Umtanum', 'Yakima Canyon'.
  • Lead singles 'Much Less Me' (Super 8 video by Jolie M-A) and 'Big Dumb World' (video by James Hyson); fall tour 2026-10-29 Los Angeles (Gold Diggers), 2026-11-06 Kingston NY (Tubby's), 2026-11-07 Ridgewood NY (Stone Circle Theatre).
  • Status as of research pass 2026-10-11: announced / pre-release — release listings this week still carry 2026-10-16 as upcoming; the archive records 'announced' until a release is confirmed.
  • Real name: Ben Funkhouser (verified — named in the Stereogum announcement interview and the Release Wave artist profile).
  • On the title 'Uncle': the artist's own framing (Stereogum interview) — saying 'uncle', giving up, as a tongue-in-cheek nod; the record reflects on the last ten years, expectations, and aging, made from a mid-30s vantage point as a best-shot project rather than a youth-lane chase.
  • Vinyl LP pre-order open through K Records (Faygoluvers).
  • Same release-day shelf 2026-10-16 (AltPress October calendar): @ – Autosmile (4AD); The Avalanches – No Bad Memories; Dummy – do you love the color of the sky?; Fontaines D.C. – Dopamine Chamber; N8NOFACE – Tomorrow's World; They Are Gutting a Body of Water – KOOL FAITH/LOOSIES II; Taking Back Sunday – Louder Now (20th Anniversary Edition).
  • 'Big Dumb World' video: posted by its maker James Logan Hyson on Vimeo — corroborating Stereogum's 'James Hyson's video' credit (single-source).
  • K Records' label copy (via upcomingvinyl): recently relocated to Los Angeles; tours the US and Europe this autumn with a full band (single-source).
  • Release Wave artist profile's comparison line: fans of Gin Blossoms, The Replacements, Yuck, and Superdrag (single-source).
  • The August 18, 2026 'new songs out today' roundup (Our Culture Mag) covers 'Much Less Me' as the lead single with Alex Farrar's production (single-source — corroborates the Stereogum announcement).
  • Listening party: Thu Oct 15, 7 PM, FREE, RSVP — re-verified live on the venue calendar (roadhouse.permanentrecordsla.com, verified). No third single, no advance reviews, no 'Uncle' interviews as of 2026-10-12 (normal 4 days out).
