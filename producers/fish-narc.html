Scene context

Evidence: mirrors data/v1/scene-figures.json (archive research pass 2026-10-11, build_circle.py) plus the wave-27 Uncle-LP diligence below. Facts reproduced here so the producer page is self-contained; the canonical copy lives in scene-figures.json.

  • Two as-credited archive productions: 'Soul Shaking' (2018-01-03) and 'Pavement' (2019-12-30), both (prod. Fish Narc).
  • Musical moniker of Ben Funkhouser; Seattle/Olympia, Washington; member of Thraxxhouse and GothBoiClique (archive scene-figures research pass).
  • Produced Lil Peep's 'Goth Angel Sinner' LP; worked with Wicca Phase Springs Eternal, Lil Tracy, Horse Head, and Cold Hart; production noted for live guitar instead of samples (archive scene-figures research pass).
  • Solo LPs: 'WiLDFiRE' (2020), 'Camouflage' (2022), 'fruiting body' [KLP295] (2023), 'frog song' (2025, K Records) (archive scene-figures research pass).
  • Fifth solo LP 'Uncle' announced (Stereogum, August 2026): 11 tracks on K Records [KLP313], produced by Asheville's Alex Farrar (Wednesday, MJ Lenderman, Fiddlehead). Singles 'Much Less Me' and 'Big Dumb World'.
  • Wave-27 diligence 2026-10-11: 'Uncle' is STILL PRE-RELEASE. Upcomingvinyl and Faygoluvers carry 2026-10-16; AltPress's October calendar lists fish narc - Uncle (K) on Oct 16; yourcountdown.to (crawled 2026-10-11) counts down to 2026-10-16.
