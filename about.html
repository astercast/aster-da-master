About

About

Aster da Master makes underground, internet-made music. He has been releasing since 2017.

Distributors file him under Hip-Hop/Rap, Alternative and Pop. The music sits in the dirtier, abstract corners of each.

A label album in 2019 (Master Key), four projects in 2020, the Cast It years of 2021 and 2022, then a run of SoundCloud-only releases from 2023 on.
