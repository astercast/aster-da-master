About

Aster Da Master is an underground, internet-native artist active since 2017. Distributors file him under Hip-Hop/Rap: and under Alternative and even Pop: but his world is the digicore underground: melodic, internet-raised, and unmistakably his own.

No interviews, press features, or official bios exist anywhere we have found, so this archive is built strictly from verified distributor and platform records. The catalog tells the story: a WoT label era in 2019 with the album Master Key; a prolific 2020 run of four projects; the Cast It: years of 2021–2022; the SomeLowLifes collective era of 2023–2024; a quiet 2025; and a comeback singles run through spring 2026.

His titles read like incantations: Cast Out, Master Key, B4 the Ritual, Solstice, Bright: lowercase chaos and emoticons where you would expect gloss. Thirty-four releases across nine years, and he keeps moving. Real name, age, and hometown are not publicly known; this site invents nothing.
