    ✦How it's built

    The merge rules

    • Sources61 sessions from data/sessions.json (enriched by session-clusters.json), 9 dated milestones from milestones.json, 44 collaboration appearances from circle.json — one event per title-credited track, release page resolved by exact title+date join.
    • OrderChronological. When several events share a date, milestones come first, then sessions, then collaborations — a stated presentation rule, not an artist fact.
    • ErasAssigned by calendar year. The April 2017 feature credit predates the era system and is labeled "Before the vault" — honestly, not forced into one.
    • The silenceThe 553-day longest silence is a span, not a moment: it sits at its start date (2022-02-22) with the end date carried on the row.
    • PlaysSingle 2026-10-11 snapshot. Distributor singles carry no vault plays — the row says so instead of showing a zero.
