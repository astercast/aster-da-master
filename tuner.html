✦How the dial works

Three moves, whole archive

  • Sweep the eras. Ten year stops, 2017–2026 — the vault years 2018–2026 plus the one 2017 feature credit that starts the record. The needle tracks the tuned entry's real date across the band.
  • Pick a format. Tracks stop only on the 185 SoundCloud-vault entries (the ones carrying play counts), singles on the 25 distributor singles, albums on the 9 long-players. Filters combine: 2020 albums is a three-stop band.
  • Every stop is a record. The card prints only what the catalog carries for that entry — plays, track counts, labels, artwork, links — and links onward to its full release page. A stop can also be shared: the dial writes its slug into the address bar as you tune.
