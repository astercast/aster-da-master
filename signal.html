✦method

How the watch works

  • Detection is keyed, not string-matched. Tracks are keyed by normalized title — case, apostrophe style and whitespace unified — or by permalink once a live refresh captures one. The first build of this watcher keyed on title-plus-date and flagged 121 of 185 tracks as new; upload dates drift between capture methods (the research capture runs a day behind api-v2 for most tracks, months for a few), so dates are display data, never identity.
  • Counts are captures, not a feed. Play counts move between captures; nothing here is live. The daily cron (GET /api/cron/signal) pulls a fresh capture, diffs it, and rewrites data/v1/signal-watch.json only when something changed. This page reads that file, or GET /api/watch where the functions are deployed.
  • Every capture is kept whole. Each pull is archived under data/captures/ in the repo, one file per capture, never edited after the fact. The ledger above is the running totals from those files (data/v1/stats-snapshots.json); any two captures can be re-diffed to check the watch's arithmetic — the test suite does exactly that on every run.
  • Rates need a real gap. A per-day figure is plain division over the time between two captures, reported only when that gap is at least 24 hours. Estimates, never predictions.
  • The date board is research, not a scrape. A person checks each date against primary listings and independent coverage, and the board keeps the disagreements on the record. It is re-swept by hand; checked_at says when.
  • Titles and metadata only. The watch deals in titles, dates, counts and venues. Nothing else is carried, quoted or inferred.
